Rules the design enforces

  • Company isolation in the database: Activities reference Accounts and ActivityTypes through (CompanyId, Id), and Submissions reference Activities the same way, so a row can never point at another company's data.
  • Change history: Accounts, Activities and Submissions are temporal tables; every change is kept in AccountsHistory, ActivitiesHistory and SubmissionsHistory.
  • Company-specific data: Accounts.ExtraFields and Submissions.Answers are JSON (checked by the database), and each key is described in CustomFieldDefinitions.
  • Traceable copy: LegacyDoctorId, LegacyTaskId and LegacyTaskFeedbackId link every copied row to its original.
